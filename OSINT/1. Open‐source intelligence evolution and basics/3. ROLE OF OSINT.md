# Role of OSINT

OSINT = collecting, analyzing, and interpreting publicly available info from open sources. It's legal, transparent, and accessible to anyone with the right tools.

OSINT is _not_ classified or proprietary. Its power comes from being open.

## Role of OSINT in Cybersecurity

- Spots emerging threats and vulnerabilities in real time
- Identifies system/software weaknesses before attackers do
- Helps understand scope and damage of a breach during incident response
- Detects phishing campaigns and data leaks via dark web monitoring

## Where OSINT Gets Used

### National Security

Governments use it to monitor adversarial nations, track terrorist networks, analyze geopolitical shifts, and coordinate disaster response. After the 2010 Haiti earthquake, OSINT data directly shaped relief coordination.

### Business Intelligence

Companies track competitors (pricing, product launches, ad campaigns), monitor brand reputation, study market trends, and assess supply chain risk. Coca-Cola uses it to watch competitors in real time. During COVID-19, companies relied on it to catch supply disruptions early.

### Cybersecurity

Threat intelligence, vulnerability assessment, incident response, phishing detection — all of these rely on OSINT as a base layer.

### Law Enforcement

Used for digital footprint analysis, social media investigations, tracking suspects, locating missing persons, and fighting human trafficking.

### Humanitarian / Disaster Response

Damage assessment after disasters, resource allocation for aid, and real-time crisis mapping using open data.

### Journalism & Academia

Investigative reporters use it to uncover stories and verify facts. Researchers use it across social sciences, political science, geography. Also used for fact-checking claims by governments or organizations.

### Competitive Intelligence

Market trend analysis, evaluating M&A targets, spotting gaps in the market for product development.

### Diplomacy

Informs foreign policy, monitors compliance with international agreements, helps predict political events like elections.

### Environment & Conservation

Tracks deforestation via satellite imagery, monitors endangered species movement, supports climate change research.

### Urban Planning

Monitors urban growth and infrastructure, supports disaster preparedness, informs land use and zoning decisions.
