## What globalization means

Globalization is the movement that allows governments, businesses, and organizations to communicate and integrate on an international scale. The internet made this possible.

Before the internet, reach was local — a garage sale, a newspaper ad at most. The internet progressively expanded that reach from neighborhood to city to country to the entire world.

## What the internet changed

- **News** — events anywhere in the world reach people instantly
- **Commerce** — buying and selling goods across cities, states, and countries
- **Entertainment** — on-demand access to more TV, music, and media than anyone could consume in a lifetime
- **Education** — online courses and an enormous library of educational resources accessible from anywhere
- **Social and political movements** — social media allows movements to gain global attention and coordinate across borders

# Internet of Things (IoT)

## What IoT is

IoT refers to everyday physical devices being connected to the internet and made "smarter" — able to sense, collect data, and respond automatically rather than requiring manual operation.

Examples:

- Smart thermostats that detect when you leave home and adjust temperature automatically
- Fridges that track what food you have inside
- Lights that adjust based on mood or time of day
- Cars that drive themselves

The core idea: take devices that used to require manual input and give them internet connectivity and intelligence.
